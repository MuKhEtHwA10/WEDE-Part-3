About Kaya Home & Living

Locally made, sustainably sourced, and crafted with care.

Rooted in South African craft

Kaya Home & Living opened its doors in 2018 as a small storefront in Johannesburg with a simple belief: that beautiful, well-made homeware should tell the story of the people who made it.

Today, we partner with over 30 artisans and small-batch producers across the country — from weavers in the Eastern Cape to potters in KwaZulu-Natal. Every piece in our store is chosen for its quality, craftsmanship, and the connection it brings to the maker's hands.

"We believe that a home should feel like it was collected over time — with pieces that have meaning, not just decoration."

Our story

Kaya — meaning "home" in many South African languages — was born from a desire to celebrate local craft and make it accessible to a wider audience. What started as a weekend market stall quickly grew into a beloved storefront, and now, an online destination.

We work directly with artisans, ensuring fair wages and long-term partnerships. Every purchase supports a maker, a family, and a tradition.

Our journey has been shaped by the relationships we've built — with the weavers, potters, and makers who pour their skill into every piece, and with the customers who bring those pieces into their homes.
